# 🚀 The 10+ LPA Cybersecurity Masterplan: 365 Days to Elite

Welcome to the definitive 365-day roadmap designed for **CyberSim**. This plan assumes you spend **2 hours daily**, combining theory, hands-on lab environments, and real-world hacking to get you job-ready for a 10+ Lac per annum salary.

This roadmap transitions you from a beginner to an elite cybersecurity professional. The structure operates as an RPG: you grind experience, complete daily challenges, and face increasingly complex AI-defended scenarios.

---

## 🛡️ Phase 1: The Foundations (Days 1–90)
> **Goal:** Understand the playground. You cannot hack what you do not understand. Networking, Linux, and scripting are the holy trinity.

### **Month 1: Networking & Reconnaissance (Days 1-30)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 1 hr Theory, 1 hr CyberSim Level 1
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   OSI & TCP/IP Models, IP Addressing, Subnetting.
    *   Protocols: HTTP, HTTPS, FTP, SSH, DNS, DHCP.
    *   **Nmap Ninja:** Host discovery, port scanning, OS fingerprinting, NSE scripts.
    *   **Wireshark:** Packet analysis, sniffing, filtering malicious traffic.
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** **Level 1 (The Network Recon)**
    *   Find the hidden ports, evade the AI defender's scan detection, and map the network.

### **Month 2: Linux Mastery (Days 31-60)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 30 min Theory, 1.5 hr CyberSim Level 2
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   Linux File Hierarchy, Basic & Advanced Commands (`grep`, `awk`, `sed`, `find`).
    *   Permissions (`chmod`, `chown`, SUID/SGID), User Management.
    *   Service Management (`systemctl`), Networking in Linux (`ip`, `netstat`, `ss`).
    *   SSH Key Management, Bash Scripting basics.
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** **Level 2 (Linux Shell Gym)**
    *   Escaping restricted shells, hunting for hidden files, and manipulating permissions.

### **Month 3: Python for Hackers (Days 61-90)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 1 hr Learning Syntax, 1 hr Writing Scripts
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   Data types, Loops, Conditionals, Functions.
    *   File I/O, Error Handling, Regular Expressions.
    *   Network Programming (`socket`, `requests` libraries).
    *   Writing custom tools (Port Scanners, Banner Grabbers, Directory Bruteforcers).
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** **Level 3 (Python Port Scanner)**
    *   Build a custom Python scanner to replace Nmap and bypass the AI's signature-based detection.

## ⚔️ Phase 2: Advancing the Arsenal (Days 91–210)
> **Goal:** Move from understanding the system to exploiting its flaws and securing it.

### **Month 4: Theory & Defensive Concepts (Days 91-120)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 2 hrs Theory & Flashcards
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   CompTIA Security+ curriculum basics.
    *   IAM (Identity and Access Management), Cryptography Basics.
    *   Risk Management, Threat Modeling.
    *   Malware types, Social Engineering vectors.
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** *Theory Days* (Read, take notes, ask the CyberSim Mentor AI questions).

### **Month 5: Privilege Escalation (Days 121-150) [NEW MODULE]**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 45 min Theory, 1 hr 15 min CyberSim Level 4
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   Linux PrivEsc techniques: SUID binaries, Cron Jobs, Sudowers bypass, Kernel Exploits.
    *   Automated enumeration (`LinPEAS`).
    *   Spawning TTY shells and reverse shells.
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** **Level 4 (Privilege Escalation)**
    *   Start as a low-privileged user `www-data` and find a path to `root`.

### **Month 6: Cryptography & Steganography (Days 151-180) [NEW MODULE]**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 1 hr Theory, 1 hr CyberSim Level 5
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   Symmetric vs Asymmetric encryption, Hashing (MD5, SHA).
    *   Cracking hashes with `JohnTheRipper` and `Hashcat`.
    *   Extracting hidden data using `steghide` and `exiftool`.
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** **Level 5 (Cryptography & Steg)**
    *   Decrypt an intercept message and find the hidden flag inside an image file.

### **Month 7: Web Application Exploitation (Days 181-210)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 1 hr OWASP Theory, 1 hr CyberSim Level 10
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   OWASP Top 10: SQL Injection, XSS, CSRF, SSRF, IDOR.
    *   Burp Suite Mastery: Proxying, Intruder, Repeater.
    *   Command Injection and File Inclusion (LFI/RFI).
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** **Level 10 (Web App Exploitation)**
    *   Hack the intentionally vulnerable Flask app while the AI Defender actively tries to block your IP and patch vulnerabilities on the fly.

## 🌍 Phase 3: The Real World (Days 211–270)
> **Goal:** Test your skills against complex, community-built machines.

### **Month 8: TryHackMe & Guided Labs (Days 211-240)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 2 hrs THM Boxes
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   Complete THM paths: "Complete Beginner", "Web Fundamentals", "Offensive Pentesting".
    *   Learn to chain multiple vulnerabilities together.
*   **CyberSim Active Level:** *External Training*

### **Month 9: HackTheBox & Blind Pentesting (Days 241-270)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 2 hrs HTB Boxes
*   **Topics Covered:**
    *   Transition to HTB. Start doing "Easy" and "Medium" machines without walkthroughs.
    *   Focus on Active Directory basics (Kerberoasting, AS-REP Roasting, BloodHound).
    *   Write detailed pentest reports for every box you crack.

## 💼 Phase 4: Professional Readiness (Days 271–365)
> **Goal:** Get the paper, build the brand, land the 10+ LPA job.

### **Month 10: Certification Grind (Days 271-300)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 2 hrs Exam Prep
*   **Focus:** Choose a certification based on your path:
    *   **eJPT / PNPT:** Great for practical pentesting.
    *   **OSCP:** The gold standard (requires immense dedication).
    *   **Security+ / CySA+:** Great for SOC Analyst / Blue Team roles.

### **Month 11: Portfolio & Bug Bounty (Days 301-330)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 1 hr Bug Bounty, 1 hr Brand Building
*   **Focus:**
    *   **Bug Bounty:** Hunt on HackerOne/Bugcrowd (Focus on VDPs initially).
    *   **Portfolio:** Document your HTB/THM write-ups on a Medium blog or GitHub pages.
    *   **Networking:** Connect on LinkedIn, share your weekly progress, engage with the infosec community.

### **Month 12: Interview Prep & Job Hunting (Days 331-365)**
*   **Daily Time Distribution:** 1 hr Applications, 1 hr Mock Interviews
*   **Focus:**
    *   Tailor your resume (Highlight Labs, CyberSim project, Bug Bounties, Certifications).
    *   Practice common interview questions (e.g., "What happens when you type google.com?", "How would you bypass a WAF for SQLi?").
    *   Apply for roles like: Junior Penetration Tester, Security Analyst, SOC Analyst L1/L2, Application Security Engineer.

### 💡 Secret Tips for the 10+ LPA Goal
1. **Never copy-paste.** Always type commands out. Build muscle memory.
2. **Read the Docs.** Don't just watch videos. Read Man pages (`man nmap`) and understand *why* a tool works.
3. **Understand the Code.** You must be able to read Python/Bash scripts to modify exploits.
4. **Use your AI Mentor.** When stuck in CyberSim, ask the AI Mentor for hints, not the direct answer.
5. **Consistency > Intensity.** 2 hours EVERY DAY is vastly superior to 14 hours on a Sunday.
